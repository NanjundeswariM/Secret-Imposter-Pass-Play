SECRET IMPOSTERPASS · WHISPER · ACCUSE
How to Play
01

One word, one secret

Everyone adds their name and picks a category. The game secretly chooses one word from it — and secretly chooses 1–2 players to be the Imposter(s).

02

Pass the phone privately

Hand the phone to each player in turn. They tap the card to reveal their role, then tap again to hide it before passing it on. Crew sees the word — Imposters see nothing.

03

Talk, without saying it

Crew members drop hints about the word to prove they know it. Imposters bluff and listen closely, trying to guess it from context. Never say the word out loud.

04

Vote together

When the timer ends (or sooner, if you're confident), the group agrees on who to accuse and selects them on the phone.

05

Reveal the truth

If the accused exactly match the real Imposter(s), the Crew wins. If even one Imposter slips through, the Imposters win.
